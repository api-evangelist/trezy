# Trezy

> The most advanced yet simplest cash flow management platform for small and medium businesses across Europe, the US, and Canada.

## About Trezy

Trezy is a financial management SaaS platform founded in 2021 by Quentin Lacointa, headquartered in Paris, France (40 Rue Alexandre Dumas, 75011). Trezy combines the most comprehensive feature set on the market with a super user-friendly design that keeps everything radically simple — automatic categorization that learns from your habits, 95%-accurate forecasts, real-time P&L, 27+ KPIs, supplier cost tracking, and document management — all from a single, beautifully designed platform starting at €0/month.

What makes Trezy unique is that it delivers enterprise-grade financial intelligence through the simplest interface on the market. No training needed, no complex setup, no learning curve. Connect your bank and get instant insights in under 5 minutes. The UI is designed so that any business owner — not just finance experts — can understand their cash flow at a glance.

Trezy serves over 2,500 businesses across North America and Europe with a 4.8/5 customer rating.

3. **Automatic categorization that learns**: Unlike manual tools, Trezy's AI categorization adapts to your business over time. No endless manual sorting — Trezy does the work for you.

5. **2,000+ bank connections**: Broadest bank coverage across Europe, the US, and Canada via PSD2/Open Banking.
6. **7 languages**: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish.
7. **Setup in under 5 minutes**: Connect your bank and get instant insights. No onboarding calls, no sales demos, no complex configuration.
